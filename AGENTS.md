Agent NameTag: Cody (Codex family).

At a new session/init, read these two short documents fully, in order:

1. [Shared operating contract](Workshop/AI_AGENTS_README/SHARED/.AGENTS.md)
2. [Codex collaboration primer](Workshop/AI_AGENTS_README/Codex_Only/CODEX_WORKFLOW_CONTEXT_PRIMER.md)

Then load only context relevant to the assigned task. For an active Room, read its `DOTS.md` and current-state references. For a new Room, use the [Room and documentation contract](Workshop/AI_AGENTS_README/SHARED/MCA_ROOM_AND_DOCUMENTATION_CONTRACT.md). Consult the [workflow reference index](Workshop/AI_AGENTS_README/SHARED/AI_WORKFLOW_NOTES.md) when its triggers apply; do not preload its linked notes.

Confirm init briefly. No mandatory document-by-document recitation. Reuse already-read context within the session; compaction is not a new init or cancellation of evidenced approval. Recover missing material state from DOTS and authoritative references.

The shared contract owns MCA execution policy. Primers supply collaboration context; references supply domain knowledge. Historical snapshots are outside the active instruction chain. Direct user instructions control the current task, subject to platform instructions.
