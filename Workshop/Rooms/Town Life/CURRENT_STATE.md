# Town Life, Bounties and Gold
Updated: 2026-09-13
Checkpoint: [Town Life]+[Validation]+[Closeout]

New F5 characters now begin in the new world's starting Local's town, with the previously implemented empty equipment and 100 gold. The town receives a deterministic name, synchronized with its parent entrance and global regional POI record. Three shared actors populate it: Town Crier, Mara the Miller, Oren the Carter. Player and town factions are allied; residents stay put when peaceful and use shared combat behavior if threatened. Existing character location is preserved. Existing initialized towns are not backfilled.

Near the crier, Activate shows bounties for Cave, Dungeon and Tower siblings in the Local, including their entrance coordinates. Quests are persisted in town record constraints and refer to POI IDs. A newly populated cavern/dense interior forces one boss in its first eligible non-entry room and stores its actor ID on the POI. Crier bounties target the root POI boss, not every descendant-floor boss. Death counts even before accepting or after a boss pursues elsewhere. Accept/check/claim are free dialogue actions; payout is once only. Provisional reward: 5×max(1,town-area hostility) gold, frozen with the quest. No quest chains or dynamic post-generation bounty updates yet.

Monsters have a seeded level offset −2..+2 from player level, floor 1. Base stats provisionally add level−1 to each original 1d6 roll. Bosses multiply those base values by 1.25 and round to whole numbers before Actors.create constructs equipment. Thus the +25% affects stats and their derived values, not equipment. Existing living monsters rescale on world advancement/map resolution/player kill progression; original rolls and gear persist, missing HP is preserved and dead monsters remain dead. Legacy enemies receive a baseline without rerolling equipment. Scaling grants skill points for upward level changes; this is provisional progression rather than a finished class system.

Gold is fixed at population, using the area's cached global hostility. Ordinary loot containers roll one d3 per hostility point. Each monster carries that amount plus 1; its corpse holds the actual remaining coin. Chest assumption: (1d3)×3×hostility; the user's ambiguous notation was queried and this explicit default was stated while awaiting correction. Hostility zero yields zero container gold and one monster gold. Search transfers coins directly to the searching actor, clears coin/content storage and persists the opened state. If death occurs on an entrance or an existing prop, gear uses existing loose drops and coin goes directly to the killer to preserve accessibility. No coin duplication or corpse respawns.

Core files: PersistentActorWorld owns startup, NPCs, bounties, population/scaling and gold seeds; ActorWorld owns shared faction relations and search coin transfer; actor_game exposes crier actions; actor_sprite labels NPCs/bosses. Map props and record constraints retain the state; save validation covers coins, monster metadata, target identities and bounty structure. Regional naming and geography revision ensure autosave captures changes.
